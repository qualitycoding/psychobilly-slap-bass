# S-005 articulation reference route

Status: **recording route defined; no articulation audio yet exists**. This file does not claim that the G-004 reference corpus has been obtained. S-025 must produce and verify the recordings before S-100.

## Lawful route

The project will create its own isolated reference recordings with a performer who signs the adjacent consent and licence grant before recording. Each accepted file must carry performer/source consent, an explicit public redistribution licence for the evidence bundle, capture notes and SHA-256. Commercial recordings by Frenzy, Hillbilly Moon Explosion or Demented Are Go remain private analysis material only and are never copied into this repository or an evidence branch.

If project-owned recording cannot be completed, S-025 and the articulation portion of G-004 block. Ordinary-pluck CC0 audio is not a substitute.

## Required capture matrix

Record at 48 kHz or 96 kHz, 24-bit, with no mastering normalisation. Capture bridge piezo, close fingerboard/contact and body/microphone paths separately where equipment permits. Include at least three usable examples of each articulation at matched pitches and approximate velocities:

- pluck: ordinary outward finger pluck;
- pop: outward pull/release with fingerboard rebound;
- slap: inward strike with pitched and contact components;
- ghost: muted inward strike with minimal sustained pitched energy.

Also record hard/soft plucks for glide fitting, sustained notes for per-partial T60, rapid same-string repetitions and rhythmic ghost/slap patterns. Keep fit and held-out takes separate. Technique-video or commercial-mix features may guide analysis but cannot populate the sealed listening bundle.

## Bundle acceptance

S-100 constructs twelve matched articulation pairs: three pluck, three pop, three slap and three ghost. Each pair uses a one-second start window, RMS matching to -23 dBFS, 24-bit WAV, sealed balanced A/B assignment and source/licence/checksum attribution. The articulation score is independent of the original ordinary-pluck score and the qualitative override remains binding.

## Privacy and repository hygiene

Private analysis audio belongs under `research/calibration/private-audio/`, which is ignored by Git. Do not commit performer addresses, signatures, private contact details or listener-identifying metadata. Commit only the unsigned consent template, capture/licensing metadata needed for evidence, derived non-audio measurements and redistribution-cleared reference assets explicitly approved for the bundle.
